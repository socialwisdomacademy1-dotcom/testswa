About us

Social Wisdom Academy, guided by Chirag Verma, was built on a simple belief: every child can excel when they are understood, encouraged and taught with patience.

We do not chase rote learning. We build strong concepts, regular habits and the courage to ask questions, so that results follow naturally and last well beyond the exam hall.

Every student who walks in is treated as an individual. Every parent is kept informed. Every success, small or large, is celebrated, because growth is made of many quiet days of effort.

A teacher plants a seed of curiosity today and watches a confident future bloom tomorrow.Our promise to every student and family
